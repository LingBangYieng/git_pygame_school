Player Briefing


***Must Read***
Hello Player
Your Goal is to Save all the Hostages without aggroing the enemies.
If a Bombwest is running at you feel free to defend yourself by clicking the Right Mouse Button.
When sight on a hostage becomes clear press E to save it.
When saving an Hostage youll have to shoot the Hostage keepers Head do this by putting your mouse against the movement of your crosshair. 
Dont shoot the hostage!

The game is optimized for 1280 x 720 resolution so when changing keep in mind that FPS drops will be part of your experience.



***voluntary***
The MapBuilder is in BETA but can be used to build your own Maps. 
The Hitbox layer will be calculöated off of the Layer = 1 so only place walls on layer 1
There are numerous button Combinations:

shift + 1 or Numpad Plus       to go up one Layer
- or minus or Numpad Minus     to go down one Layer
            r                  to rotate current sprite
            g                  to draw the Grid
            p                  to draw a preview of what youll place
            ctrl + s           to save to Hard disk, switch to command line or terminal to enter filename and press enter
            ctrl + o           to open save from Hard disk, switch to command line or terminal to enter filename and press enter then switch back again
            0                  to wipe the whole screen
